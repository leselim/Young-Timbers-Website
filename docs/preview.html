The founder

Leseli Morakile

Leseli started Young Timbers in 2019, before the company had a name worth printing. The work came first: a brand that needed rebuilding, a system that needed fixing, a business that needed to look like itself again.

Leseli works across brand, product and code, and does not draw hard lines between them. That range set the company's direction: brand and engineering treated as one discipline rather than two departments, and senior attention on the work from the first conversation to handover.

Young Timbers is named for what it is: something young, growing, and built to hold weight.
